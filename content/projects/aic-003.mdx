## Requirements

- Ingest Markdown ADRs and runbooks from repositories.
- Preserve document structure (title, status, date, decision) as metadata.
- Filter by status — superseded decisions should be clearly marked.

## Research

Open questions being investigated:

1. Chunk by heading or by fixed size for long runbooks?
2. Does hybrid search (keyword + vector) outperform pure vector search for short, jargon-heavy queries?
3. How should superseded ADRs be ranked?

To be decided after the research spike. Candidates: extend AIC-001's index, or a separate index with its own schema.
